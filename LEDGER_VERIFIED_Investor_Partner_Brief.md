## The Problem

High-value assets—farm equipment, marine vessels, aircraft, fleet vehicles, medical and industrial machinery, luxury automobiles, commercial property—share the same failure mode:

**Critical history lives in heads, receipts in shoeboxes, and seller stories at closing.**

- Maintenance is performed (or claimed) without **spec verification**—wrong oil, wrong interval, wrong part tier.
- Records are **inconsistent, editable, or missing** when resale, insurance, or litigation arrives.
- Buyers discount price for **uncertainty**; insurers price **unknown risk**; owners cannot prove **premium care**.
- Skilled trades lose status to imported labor while **documented craft** goes unrewarded.

The market does not lack service providers. It lacks a **neutral, durable standard** for what counts as proof—and providers who **perform to that standard** in the field.

## The Solution: Ledger-verified

### The mark

**Ledger-verified** is the seal applied only when an event passes a **defined standard pack**:

- Correct **interval** (hours, miles, cycles, calendar)
- Correct **materials** (grade, spec, approved tier—not “some oil from a shelf”)
- Correct **procedure** (checklist, measurements, tests where required)
- Correct **provider** (certified org and technician)
- Correct **evidence** (timestamp, documentation, chain of custody, immutable storage)

If it does not pass, **it is not on the trusted ledger.** Self-reporting is not Ledger-verified.

### The two layers (both required)

| Layer | Role |
|--------|------|
| **Standard & ledger** | Rules, validation, asset registry, reports, search, institutional presentation |
| **Certified execution** | Expert partners who **demonstrate** the standard—premium tools, premium materials, premium outcomes |

We are the **Ritz concierge**, not the Holiday Inn front desk: same function as “service,” different client, different proof, different outcome.

**Coffee cup truth:** *We're not the front desk. We're the concierge. Same function. Different standard.*

## Platform Architecture: Start at Stage 5

We refuse the trap of “Phase 1 spreadsheets, Phase 3 platform.” **The data model is final on day one.** Early events may leave fields empty; **columns never appear later that should have existed from the first record.**

### Core entities (relational, durable)

1. **Asset** — permanent ID; class (marine, ag, fleet, property, medical, etc.); value tier; survives **ownership transfer**  
2. **Standard pack** — versioned rules per asset class (intervals, materials, procedures, evidence)  
3. **Certified provider** — organizations and technicians authorized to submit events  
4. **Event** — inspection, maintenance, repair, attestation, amendment; **append-only**  
5. **Evidence object** — files, photos, readings, hashes; **tamper-evident**  
6. **Verification outcome** — pass / fail; only **pass** enters trusted history  
7. **Report** — buyer, insurer, owner resale packet—**derived from verified events only**

### Principles

- **Corrections = amendment events**, not silent edits  
- **AI assists capture** (voice, photo → structured data); **humans and rules attest**  
- **No advertising on reports** — authority, not ad clutter  
- **Pay-per-report** for third parties on high-value assets—fees that match ticket size
