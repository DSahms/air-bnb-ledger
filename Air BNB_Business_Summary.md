## What PropertyLedger Does

PropertyLedger is a subscription-based property condition management service targeting short-term rental (Airbnb, VRBO) owners in the greater Philadelphia, South Jersey, Atlantic City, and Pocono Mountains markets. The service provides professional between-tenant walkthroughs, minor cosmetic repairs, and — critically — a permanent, timestamped, photographic condition record for every property enrolled in the program.

PropertyLedger is built for the owner whose most valuable asset is time. Managing turnover between guests — documenting damages, tracking wear, coordinating minor repairs — is time-consuming, inconsistent, and frequently undocumented. PropertyLedger handles all of it, and creates a verifiable record in the process.

## The Service: What's Included

### The Walkthrough

After every tenant departure and before every new arrival, a PropertyLedger representative conducts a structured walkthrough of the property. Every walkthrough produces:

* Timestamped, GPS-tagged photographs of all rooms and surfaces
* A standardized written condition report using consistent terminology across all properties
* A documented comparison between the outgoing tenant's condition and the incoming baseline
* A chain-of-custody record identifying the representative, date, and time of inspection

### Minor Repairs — Bundled in the Subscription

Cosmetic and minor maintenance items identified during the walkthrough are handled on the spot, included in the monthly subscription fee. Bundled services include:

* Light bulb and fixture replacements
* Touch-up paint on walls, trim, and corners
* Minor drywall repairs (e.g., doorknob holes, small nicks)
* Bathroom fixture adjustments (mirror resets, towel bar tightening)
* Carpet spot treatment for minor stains
* General cosmetic corrections that fall within the monthly service threshold

### Work Above the Threshold — Fair Bid, No Obligation

Any repair or replacement exceeding the bundled service threshold is bid transparently and competitively. The property owner is under no obligation to use PropertyLedger for that work. If a competing contractor comes in lower, they get the job. PropertyLedger's value is the ongoing relationship and the record — not a monopoly on repairs.

### Winterization \& Seasonal Services

Seasonal winterization (shutting down water systems, protecting pipes, securing the property) is always scoped and priced as a separate line item. It is never bundled into the monthly subscription. This applies primarily to the Atlantic City and South Jersey coastal markets, which experience significant off-season closures. The Pocono Mountains market operates year-round and does not require the same seasonal protocols.

## Pricing Structure

PropertyLedger operates on an annual contract, billed monthly. A flat annual rate eliminates seasonal pricing friction, provides the owner with predictable costs, and protects the continuity of the property record. A gap in documentation weakens the archive's value; the annual commitment ensures the record stays intact.

* **Monthly subscription:** $300–$350 per property
* **Annual commitment:** $3,600–$4,200 per property
* **Minor repair threshold:** included up to the monthly subscription value
* **Winterization:** separate bid, always outside the subscription
* **Major repairs:** fair bid, no obligation to use PropertyLedger

## The Long-Term Vision: The Dun \& Bradstreet of Short-Term Rentals

The walkthrough service is the foundation. The archive is the asset. From day one, PropertyLedger is designed not just as a maintenance company but as a property intelligence platform — the authoritative, verifiable condition record for the short-term rental market.

Every property in the PropertyLedger system builds a record that nobody else can replicate. You cannot fake a four-year timestamped photographic history. That archive becomes increasingly valuable over time in three specific ways:

### 1\. For Property Owners: Asset Value \& Leverage

A short-term rental with a documented maintenance and condition history is a fundamentally different asset than one without. Like a vehicle with a complete service record, it commands greater confidence from buyers, justifies a higher asking price, and removes ambiguity from the transaction. PropertyLedger makes that record possible — and makes it portable.

### 2\. For Insurers: Compliance \& Claims Documentation

Insurance carriers face significant exposure with short-term rental properties. The absence of documentation is the enemy of a clean claim. PropertyLedger's records provide insurers with verifiable pre-loss condition evidence, tenant-by-tenant condition comparisons, and a timestamped maintenance history. This positions PropertyLedger as a potential compliance and underwriting tool for insurers operating in the STR market.

### 3\. For Buyers \& Investors: Due Diligence on Demand

As the PropertyLedger archive grows, it becomes searchable. An investor considering the purchase of a short-term rental property can pull a PropertyLedger report the same way a car buyer pulls a Carfax. What was the condition between tenants? What repairs were made, and when? Were there recurring issues? The record answers those questions with documented evidence rather than seller representations.

### The Platform Model

The searchable PropertyLedger platform is a Phase 2 build, launched once the archive has sufficient density to be meaningful to outside parties. The platform will operate on a pay-per-report model — a modest per-search fee, no advertising, no banner monetization. The goal is an authoritative, clean information resource that commands professional trust:

* No advertising. No banner ads. No clutter.
* Pay-per-report access for buyers, investors, and insurers
* Institutional presentation: authoritative, neutral, fact-based
* The property is the anchor record — history survives ownership changes

## Infrastructure Philosophy

PropertyLedger is built for scale from the first property. The data architecture is designed with the platform in mind — not retrofitted after the fact. Key principles:

* **Standardized condition categories from day one** — every rep uses the same terminology so the archive is searchable and comparable
* **GPS and timestamp metadata embedded in every photograph automatically**
* **Property as the anchor record** — owner changes don't break the history chain
* **Chain of custody on every walkthrough** — who was there, when, and what they found
* **Field representatives focus on the work** — the data architecture is managed from the office
