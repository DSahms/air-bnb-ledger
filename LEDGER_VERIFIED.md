## Architecture principle (Phase 5 on day one)

- **Asset ID** survives owner changes  
- **Events** append-only; corrections = amendment events  
- **Standard version** on every accepted event  
- **Fields exist before values** — nullable OK, missing columns never  
- **Verification gate** — fail spec, no Ledger-verified stamp
